## 1. Introduction

Welcome to **AI Metaverse Ecosystem** ("the Application", "we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it through transparent data practices. This Privacy Policy describes how the Application operates, the types of information we handle, and your rights as a user.

By using AI Metaverse Ecosystem, you agree to the practices outlined in this policy.

---

## 2. Information We Do Not Collect

AI Metaverse Ecosystem is architected as an offline-capable, client-side application. We prioritize user privacy and minimalism:

- **No Personal Identifiable Information (PII):** We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, home addresses, or social security numbers.
- **No User Account Requirement:** You do not need to create an account, register, or provide login credentials to explore virtual worlds, simulate asset trading, or view live events.
- **No Location Tracking:** We do not track, collect, or store your GPS or precise geographical location data.
- **No Biometric Data:** We do not access facial recognition, fingerprint data, or body tracking sensors.
- **No Camera or Microphone Access:** The Application does not record audio, video, or camera streams.
- **No Contacts or Media Files:** We do not read your device contacts, photo library, or personal files.

---

## 3. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

All game and simulation progress, customized settings, inventory of virtual assets, selected worlds, and dashboard preferences are stored exclusively on your device using client-side storage mechanisms (such as `localStorage`):

- **Virtual World Preferences & Selected Realms**
- **Simulated Asset Inventory & Transaction History**
- **Bookmarked Metaverse Events & Reminders**
- **User Interface Preferences (Theme, Audio toggles, Tab state)**

This data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to our servers. You can clear this data at any time by clearing the Application's cache or storage in your device settings.

## 4. Simulated Digital Assets & In-App Economy

The digital assets, currencies, tokens, and NFT-style virtual items displayed within the Application are purely simulated and intended for entertainment, simulation, and educational purposes. No real monetary transactions or real cryptocurrency exchanges take place unless explicitly conducted through authorized, compliant third-party billing providers (e.g., Google Play In-App Billing).

## 5. Third-Party Services and Analytics

The core application functions independently of external data collection. However, if third-party application platforms or advertising networks (such as Google Play Services or AdMob) are integrated in distributed builds, they may automatically collect standard diagnostic or advertising information:

- Device model, operating system version, and general system performance data.
- Anonymous Advertising Identifier (e.g., Google Advertising ID), subject to your device privacy controls and permissions.
- Crash and diagnostic logs to help improve application stability.

These third-party service providers have their own independent privacy policies governing their collection and processing of information.

## 6. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR Compliance)

AI Metaverse Ecosystem is designed for a general audience and does not knowingly collect any personal data from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in applicable jurisdictions). If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately at tshiajan@gmail.com, and we will take immediate steps to remove such information.
